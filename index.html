Conditions Treated

Comprehensive surgical and non-surgical care for the entire upper extremity.

Hand & Fingers

  • Trigger finger
  • Dupuytren's contracture
  • Tendon injuries (flexor & extensor)
  • Fractures & dislocations
  • Thumb arthritis
  • Ganglion cysts & other masses
  • Infections

Wrist

  • Distal radius fractures
  • Scaphoid fractures
  • TFCC injuries
  • Wrist arthritis & fusion
  • Wrist arthroscopy
  • Carpal tunnel syndrome
  • De Quervain's tenosynovitis
  • Ganglion cysts

Elbow

  • Tennis & golfer's elbow
  • Cubital tunnel syndrome
  • Elbow fractures
  • Elbow stiffness & contracture
  • Distal biceps rupture
  • Elbow arthroscopy

Shoulder

  • Shoulder replacement (anatomic & reverse)
  • Rotator cuff repair
  • Shoulder instability & labral repair
  • Frozen shoulder
  • Clavicle & proximal humerus fractures
  • AC joint reconstruction

Nerve & Microsurgery

  • Nerve release procedures
  • Nerve repair, grafting & transfers
  • Tendon transfers
  • Brachial plexus injuries
  • Replantation & flap coverage

Detailed education handouts for these conditions are available on the Patient Resources page.
