About Dr. Oosten

Dr. James D. Oosten is an orthopedic surgeon specializing in conditions of the hand, wrist, forearm, elbow, and shoulder. With a foundation in bioengineering and biomechanics from the University of Pittsburgh and his medical degree from The Ohio State University College of Medicine, Dr. Oosten brings a unique engineering-informed perspective to upper extremity surgery.

One thing Dr. Oosten will never take for granted is the opportunity granted to him by his childhood orthopedic surgeons. Born with severely deformed bilateral clubfoot, he spent considerable time with orthopedic providers throughout his youth — an experience that sparked a lifelong fascination with musculoskeletal anatomy and the field of orthopedic biomechanics. Coupled with a passion for working with his hands developed through DIY projects and engineering, he became set on a career in orthopedic surgery from an early age.

His practice encompasses the full spectrum of upper extremity conditions, from acute traumatic injuries such as fractures and tendon lacerations to chronic conditions including arthritis, nerve compression, and complex reconstructive cases. Dr. Oosten is committed to providing expert, compassionate surgical care to help patients restore function and return to the activities they love.

As a researcher with publications in sports medicine, hand surgery, biomechanics, and basic science, Dr. Oosten believes that a firm understanding of the science governing implant and repair stability can help optimize orthopedic interventions and improve treatment outcomes.

Patient Experience Philosophy

Every patient deserves a surgeon who listens carefully, explains thoroughly, and partners with them in their recovery.

Patient-Centered Care

I believe in treating the whole person, not just the injury. Each treatment plan is tailored to your unique goals, lifestyle, and needs.

Clear Communication

Understanding your condition is essential to recovery. I take the time to explain your diagnosis, treatment options, and what to expect every step of the way.

Evidence-Based Treatment

My approach combines the latest research and surgical techniques with proven clinical experience to deliver the best possible outcomes.

Collaborative Recovery

Optimal outcomes require teamwork. I work closely with hand therapists, rehabilitation specialists, and your primary care team to support your complete recovery.
