Insurances Accepted

Coverage varies by plan. A few minutes on the phone with your insurer before the visit prevents most billing surprises.

Verifying Your Coverage

Please contact your insurance provider to determine what coverage is available for you to see this provider.

Ask specifically about specialist visit co-pays, your deductible, and whether a referral or prior authorization is required.

A specific carrier list will be added once in practice.

What to Bring

  • Insurance card and photo ID
  • Referral, if your plan requires one
  • Current medication list
  • Imaging discs and reports
  • Prior operative notes and EMG results

Arrive 15 minutes early to complete registration.

Authorization for Procedures

MRI, CT, injections, and surgery frequently require prior authorization. That process is started by the office after your visit and can take several business days.

Workers’ compensation and personal injury claims require additional documentation — bring your claim number and adjuster contact information.

Before your appointment, review Preparing for Your Visit.
